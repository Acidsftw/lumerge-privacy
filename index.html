Lumerge – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Lumerge is a mobile game by Acids Studio (Jan Saurer, Germany). This page explains which data the game handles and why. Contact: acidsstudio@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • You play without an account. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • Your progress (level, coins, stars, village, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob and offers optional purchases through Google Play. These Google services process some device data, as described below.
  • We do not sell your data and we run no servers of our own that receive it.

2. Data stored on your device

Game progress and settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved in the app's local storage. They never leave your device through us. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, this progress is deleted.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded video ads that you start yourself and, from later levels on, occasional full-screen ads between levels. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC).

DataPurpose
Advertising ID, other device identifiersShowing ads, limiting how often an ad is shown, fraud prevention, measuring ads

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your choice with Google's consent dialog before any ad is requested. Personalised ads are shown only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. More information: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from apps that use its services.

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional in-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only receives the information whether a purchase succeeded, so that it can give you the item. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment itself.

5. What we do not do

  • No account, no login, no contact list, no photos, no microphone, no precise location.
  • No analytics or tracking SDK of our own.
  • No selling or renting of data.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are requested with a content rating limit suitable for a general audience with parental guidance. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we will help.

7. Legal basis and your rights (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies: ads and their data use are based on your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR); delivering purchases is based on the contract with you (Art. 6 (1) (b)). You have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to complain to a data protection authority. Because we store no personal data ourselves, requests about ad data are best sent to Google directly; we will gladly help: acidsstudio@gmail.com.

8. Deleting your data

Game progress: uninstall the game or clear the app data in your Android settings. Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

9. Changes

If the game gets new features that handle data differently, this page is updated and the date above changes.
